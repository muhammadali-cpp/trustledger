Built for money between people who trust each other.

Simple on the surface. Careful underneath.

Works without internet

Add a loan on a weak connection or none at all. TrustLedger saves it on your phone and uploads it automatically when your signal returns. Nothing is lost, nothing is entered twice.

Syncs on its own

Everyone sees the same numbers. Sync runs automatically while the app is open, and the status strip shows whether you're online and what's still waiting to upload.

Fair group splits

Equal splits are exact to the paisa, so shares always add up to the total. Prefer custom amounts? Enter them and the app checks the sum.

Proof of payment

Attach a screenshot when you ask to settle. The lender approves or denies, and the history stays on record.

Settle everything at once

See all loans and split shares with one person, check the total, and clear them together.

Checked on the server

Amounts, people and permissions are verified by the server, never trusted blindly from a phone.

Announcements

Important notices appear as a clear card on your home screen, with an inbox to read them later.

Private or public

Choose who can see each loan. Personal loans stay between the two of you.

Make it yours.

Nine accent colours, light and dark themes, and a choice of app icons. Tap a colour to preview it on this page.

Install in under a minute.

TrustLedger is shared directly as an APK file, so Android asks you to allow the install once.

Download

Tap the download button and save TrustLedger.apk to your phone.

Open the file

Open it from your notifications or the Downloads folder.

Allow the install

If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install apps, then go back and tap Install.

Create your account

Open TrustLedger, sign up, add your email, and invite your friends.

Google Play Protect may show a notice because the app isn't from the Play Store. Choose Install anyway to continue. Later updates are delivered inside the app.

Questions, answered.

Is TrustLedger free?

Yes. There are no fees and no ads.

Does it move real money?

No. TrustLedger is a record-keeper. You pay each other the way you already do, then mark the loan as settled in the app.

What happens if I have no internet?

You can keep recording loans and splits. They're saved on your phone and uploaded automatically once you're online.

How do updates work?

When a new version is available the app tells you and updates from inside the app. Your data stays safe, and you never need to come back to this page.

Can other people see my loans?

Only what you choose to share. Personal loans are visible to you and the other person. Public ones are visible to the group.

Which phones are supported?

Android 7.0 and newer.
